## THE MOCKUP HOOK — Execution Guide

The single highest-converting action. Use for your top 10–20 targets per week.

**Steps:**
1. Find a business with no website or a bad one
2. Open shaydefy-v2, swap hero copy + colors to match their brand (~30 min)
3. Screenshot the hero at 1440px wide
4. Send simultaneously as:
   - Touch 3 email with the screenshot attached
   - Instagram DM Variant C with the screenshot
5. Subject: `Built something for [Name]`

**Why it works:**
- You've already done the work — creates obligation to respond
- It's personalised to their specific business
- It proves competence instead of claiming it
- Hard to ignore vs. a text-only cold message

**Expected reply rate:** 20–40% (vs ~5% text-only)

International

We ship worldwide. International orders are shipped via USPS Priority Mail International or USPS First Class Package International Service depending on the destination country and order value.

International transit times vary by country — typically 7–21 business days. Customs duties, taxes, and import fees are the responsibility of the recipient. We are not able to mark packages as "gifts" or undervalue items on customs forms.

Signature Required

Due to the value of our stones, a signature is required on all deliveries. Please ensure someone will be available to receive the package at the delivery address. If you miss the delivery, USPS will leave a notice and attempt redelivery or hold the package at your local post office.

Shipping Days

We ship on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. Orders placed before 12pm PT on a shipping day will typically go out the same day. Orders placed after 12pm PT or on non-shipping days will ship on the next scheduled day.

If you need your order urgently, please contact us before placing it — we will do our best to accommodate.

Order Processing

Orders typically take 1 business day to process and verify before shipping. For large or high-value orders, Petr may reach out directly to confirm details before dispatch.

Insurance & Loss

Every order is insured for its full purchase value. In the unlikely event of loss or damage during transit, please contact us immediately and we will file a claim and replace or refund your order. Do not discard any packaging materials until the claim is resolved.

Returns & Refunds

Return Window

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. The item must be in its original, unaltered condition — unworn, undamaged, and with all original packaging included.

How to Return

  1. Email us at moldavitefamily@gmail.com with your order number and the reason for return.
  2. We will respond within one business day with a return authorization and shipping instructions.
  3. Pack the item securely in its original packaging. We recommend insuring the return shipment — we are not responsible for items lost or damaged in transit back to us.
  4. Ship to: Green Snowflake LLC, 567 W Channel Islands Blvd. #112, Port Hueneme, CA 93041
  5. Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund will be issued to your original payment method within 3–5 business days.

Return Shipping Cost

Return shipping is the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to our error (wrong item sent, item damaged in transit, or authenticity concern).

Non-Returnable Items

  • Custom or special-order pieces
  • Items that have been worn, altered, or repaired outside our workshop
  • Items returned after the 14-day window
  • Digital downloads or gift cards

Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. If you would like a different stone or item, please return the original item for a refund and place a new order.

Damaged or Wrong Item

If your item arrives damaged or we made an error with your order, please email us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. We will resolve the issue at no cost to you — either by replacement or full refund including return shipping.

Authenticity Guarantee

Every stone we sell is guaranteed genuine moldavite. If you have concerns about the authenticity of your piece, please contact us. We stand behind every item we sell with 38 years of reputation.

Common Questions

Quick Answers

Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?

Email us as soon as possible at moldavitefamily@gmail.com. If the order hasn't shipped yet, we can usually update the address. Once shipped, address changes are not possible.

Do you ship to P.O. Boxes?

Yes, USPS delivers to P.O. Boxes. However, due to signature requirements, we recommend using a residential or business address where someone can sign for the package.

Will I have to pay customs duties on my international order?

Import duties and taxes are set by your country's customs authority and are the buyer's responsibility. We are required to accurately declare the full value of all shipments on customs forms.

My tracking shows delivered but I haven't received my package.

First, check with neighbors and your building's front desk or mail room. Then contact your local USPS office with the tracking number. If the package is still not found after 48 hours, email us and we will file an insurance claim.

How do I know my moldavite is genuine?

Our family has been supplying genuine Czech moldavite since 1987. Every stone is sourced directly from the Czech Republic. We offer a full authenticity guarantee — if you have any doubts after receiving your stone, contact us.

Can I get a certificate of authenticity?

Yes. For high-value pieces and jewelry, we include a certificate of authenticity. If you would like a certificate for a raw stone purchase, please mention it in the order notes and we will include one.

Questions? We're here.

Marianne and Petr reply within the same business day. Don't hesitate to reach out before or after your purchase.

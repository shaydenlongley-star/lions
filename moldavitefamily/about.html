The Science

What is
Moldavite?

Moldavite is the product of a meteorite colliding with Earth nearly 15 million years ago. It fell over what is now called the Moldau River Valley in the Czech Republic.

These green gems are among the rarest minerals on Earth. They have been sought after for thousands of years, and often given as gifts amongst royalty. It is believed that the green stone in the legend of the Holy Grail was, in fact, moldavite.

Moldavite's supply is finite — it exists only where that meteorite struck. Every year, fewer remain.

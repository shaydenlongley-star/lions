Chapter I

The
Impact

15 million years ago, a meteorite the size of a mountain tore through the atmosphere and slammed into the European landmass at 20 kilometres per second. The energy released was greater than all nuclear weapons ever detonated — combined.

FIRE
Chapter II

The
Transformation

The collision vaporised rock and earth in an instant. The superheated material was blasted miles into the atmosphere. As it fell back to Earth, it cooled into aerodynamic shapes — twisted, sculpted, alive. The result: a glass born from the union of meteor and Earth.

BOHEMIA
Chapter III

The
Valley

The stones fell across what is now called the Moldau River Valley in the Czech Republic — Bohemia. This is the only place on Earth where they exist. Named for the valley they call home, Moldavite is a stone that belongs to one land and one land only.

ROYALS
Chapter IV

The
Royals

For thousands of years, humans who encountered these strange green stones knew they were different. Moldavite has been found at archaeological sites dating back 25,000 years. It was traded across continents, worn by shamans, gifted among kings. The rarest things always find the most important hands.

LEGEND
Chapter V

The Holy
Grail

Medieval legend speaks of a sacred cup carved from a fallen green stone — an object of divine power sought by knights across the known world. Many scholars believe the green stone at the heart of the Holy Grail legend was moldavite. The stone that fell from the sky, already holy before anyone named it.

Privacy Policy

Effective January 2025 · Green Snowflake LLC (Moldavite Family)

What We Collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, email address, shipping address, and order details. This information is necessary to fulfil your order and send you shipping updates. If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address only. We do not collect payment card details — these are handled directly by our payment processor and never pass through our servers.

How We Use It

We use your information solely to process and fulfil your orders, send order confirmation and shipping update emails, and — if you have opted in — to send occasional newsletters about new arrivals and special pieces. We do not use your data for advertising profiling, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party under any circumstances.

Third-Party Processors

We use BigCommerce as our e-commerce platform, which processes and stores order data on our behalf. Payment processing is handled by PayPal and Stripe — both are PCI-compliant and we never see your full card number. We use Google Analytics for anonymous, aggregated site usage data (pages visited, session duration) to improve the site experience. You can opt out of Google Analytics using the browser extension available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Your Rights

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to ask us to correct inaccurate data, or to request deletion of your data. To exercise any of these rights, email us at moldavitefamily@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

Our site uses essential cookies required for the shopping cart and checkout to function. We also use Google Analytics cookies, which are non-essential. No personal data is stored in cookies.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact Marianne Zdrazil at moldavitefamily@gmail.com or write to Green Snowflake LLC, 567 W Channel Islands Blvd #112, Port Hueneme, CA 93041.
